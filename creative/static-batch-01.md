# Static Image Batch 01 — Image Ad Concepts

Image ads are the cheapest test surface in a quiz funnel: near-zero production cost
(Nano Banana Pro in Higgsfield ≈ 2 credits, or Canva), instant iteration, and text-card
hooks frequently beat video on cost-per-QuizStart. Run them in the same ad sets as the
video batch and let Meta allocate.

**Compliance:** Meta penalizes heavy text overlays less than it used to, but keep cards
clean; same invite-don't-diagnose rules as video. No AI-person disclosure needed when no
photorealistic humans appear — concepts 1–6 are people-free by design.

## Concepts

### S1 — The weekday question card
Plain bold text on solid color (brand palette): **"What day of the week were you born?"**
small sub-line: *"It matters more than your star sign. Free 2-min reading."*
- Variants: black/white minimal; handwritten-note style; iPhone Notes-app screenshot style (native feel).

### S2 — Seven weekdays grid
7 tiles (Mon–Sun), each with a one-word personality teaser, one tile highlighted:
**"Which one are you? Most people have never checked."**
- Also build as a **carousel** (one card per weekday) — carousels are strong for quiz funnels.

### S3 — Credibility card
University/press framing, editorial layout: **"The only astrology methodology cleared by
a university medical-research ethics board."** Sub: *As featured on Fox, NBC, ABC.*
- Best for retargeting and the skeptic angle ad set.

### S4 — Quiz mockup
Phone mockup showing the diagnostic mid-question with a visible progress bar:
**"2 minutes. No signup. Uncomfortably accurate."**
- Closest visual match to the post-click experience = strong CTR-to-completion consistency.

### S5 — Floor-plan card (feng shui angle)
Simple apartment floor plan with one room circled in red:
**"Every home has one room nobody uses. Yours is predictable."**
Sub: *Free 2-minute home reading.*

### S6 — The split system card
Three-column minimal graphic — PERSON / PLACE / TIMING — with:
**"Every other system reads one of these. This one reads all three."**

### S7 — Founder photo card (real photo, not AI)
Gut & Non, candid teaching photo, caption-style text:
**"Two teachers. One 2,000-year-old Thai system. 3,000+ students."**
- Retargeting layer; pairs with the D1 community offer.

## Copy blocks (mix-and-match with any concept)
- Primary: "Most people can tell you their star sign. Almost nobody can tell you the day of the week they were born — the one thing Thai Taksa astrology starts with. Free 2-minute reading, no signup."
- Primary (short): "You can keep guessing. Or you can know. Free 2-min reading."
- Headlines: "What Day Were You Born?" · "Read Your Home in 2 Minutes" · "Astrology That Survived an Ethics Board" · "Join Free for 7 Days"

## Production
1. Generate S1–S6 via Nano Banana Pro (Higgsfield) or Canva brand templates; 2–3 visual variants each, 1:1 and 4:5 crops (+ 9:16 for Stories placement).
2. S7 needs a real founder photo — request from Gut & Non.
3. Naming: `static-[concept]-[variant]-[date]`. Launch inside the existing angle ad sets, not separate campaigns.
