- **CTA:** "It's free, no signup — destiny.ac. Find your weekday first, it takes ten seconds."
- **On-screen text:** "What weekday were you born? 👀" → "2-min free reading"
- **Meta primary text:** Most people can tell you their star sign. Almost nobody can tell you the one thing Thai astrology starts with: the day of the week you were born. Free 2-minute reading, no signup.
- **Headline:** What Day Were You Born? It Matters More Than Your Sign
